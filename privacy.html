Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

1. What this policy covers

This policy describes the Awei Video Publisher macOS prototype and its accompanying informational website. It does not govern the privacy practices of TikTok, Google, YouTube, OpenAI or GitHub.

2. Information used by the App

  • Local videos and drafts: Video files chosen by you and the titles, descriptions, captions, hashtags, and settings you enter.
  • Platform credentials: OAuth authorization tokens and developer API secrets needed to perform authorized actions.
  • Review exports: The App saves the selected filename, video details and draft copy to the text file you choose.
  • Technical diagnostics: Local app startup and error information used for troubleshooting.

3. Where information is stored

Video files remain on your Mac until you initiate an upload. Application Support stores settings (including the Google OAuth client file path and TikTok client key) and upload history, including a file fingerprint and platform upload identifiers. The Google OAuth configuration file stays where you selected it. The App stores platform tokens and the saved TikTok client secret in the macOS Keychain. The App developer does not operate an intermediate video-upload server.

4. When information leaves your device

If you confirm an upload, the selected video and related publishing information are transmitted to the relevant platform’s official API. For TikTok inbox uploads, the App sends the video and you add the caption in TikTok. For direct posts it also sends the caption and selected posting settings. Review exports reach an outside service only if you share them yourself. Authorization requests involve Google or TikTok as applicable.

5. Sharing and tracking

The App does not intentionally sell user data or add an independent advertising-tracking service. This static website has no custom analytics, advertising pixels, or account-registration forms. GitHub Pages, as the website host, may process technical request information under its own policies.

6. Retention and controls

To clear local data, remove the AweiShortVideoPublisher folder in your user Library/Application Support, the corresponding AweiShortVideoPublisher Keychain entries, and any review exports you saved. Manage your Google OAuth configuration file separately. Revoking access in platform settings stops future authorized calls. Local files and previously uploaded posts require separate deletion. Content uploaded to third-party platforms is subject to those platforms’ retention and deletion policies.

7. Security

We use platform authorization and the device’s credential-storage facilities, but no system is completely risk-free. Do not share developer secrets, OAuth token files, or passwords. Keep your Mac updated and revoke unused permissions.

8. Third-party services

For details on their own processing and retention practices, see the privacy policies of TikTok, Google, OpenAI, and GitHub.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy if app capabilities or data flows change. Questions and data requests: tongxuechen082@gmail.com.
