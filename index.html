macOS • Video workflow

Make publishing short videos simpler.

Awei Video Publisher is an independent macOS utility prototype that helps creators prepare publishing copy and submit videos to their own connected YouTube and TikTok accounts after confirmation.

Current status: In development, with account integration subject to each platform’s permissions and review. This website describes the software; it does not itself upload or host videos.

What the app does

Prepare publishing text

Draft platform-specific titles, descriptions, captions and hashtags. Export a local text file for manual review. You choose whether to share it with another service.

Connect your accounts

Use authorization flows provided by Google and TikTok. Users retain control of their account access and may revoke authorization.

Review before uploading

Users review publishing settings and approve uploads. Inbox uploads send the video to TikTok for completion in its app. Copy and paste your caption there. Direct posting sends the caption and selected settings when the account has permission.

How it works

Choose a local video → prepare YouTube and TikTok copy → review the selected settings → authorize the requested upload. Files stay on the Mac until an upload is initiated to the corresponding platform.
